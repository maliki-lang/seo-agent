## The trap nobody explains

Heels shift 70–80% of your body weight onto your forefoot — that's the finding from [Singapore's PSC Women and Children clinic](https://pscwomenandchildren.sg/blog/womens-foot-pain-singapore/), and it explains the forefoot pain and toe compression that build through an eight-hour day. The safe heel height is 2–4 cm. Above that, you're overloading the front of your foot beyond what it was built to carry.

So you switch to flats. The rational response — and almost everyone does it.

What nobody explains: daily heel-wearing gradually shortens your Achilles tendon, which adapts to the elevated position. When you switch to flat shoes, the Achilles is suddenly being stretched beyond its adapted length with every step. The pain that follows isn't from the flats. It's from a tendon that heels already changed.

Rachel works in a Raffles Place bank. She wore 6 cm heels for three years. When a podiatrist told her to switch to flats, she lasted two weeks — the flat shoe pain felt worse than the heels. Her Achilles had shortened from years of elevation; switching cold stretched it past its tolerance. Neither the heels nor the flats were working, because years of heels had structurally changed what her foot could handle.

The solution isn't flats. It's the 2–4 cm range — kitten heels or low block heels that offload the forefoot without demanding a fully flat position from a shortened Achilles.

## Why flat shoes aren't the safe option you think

Even without years of heels, ballet flats have their own problem. A Singapore podiatrist put it plainly: "without support, flats are simply heels without height." Dr. Hillary Brenner, a podiatric physician, describes the internal structure of most ballet flats as equivalent to cardboard. Without arch support, every step causes overpronation — the foot rolls inward, flattening the arch and sending strain up through the knee and hip. On Singapore's marble floors, MRT platforms, and concrete corridors, there is nothing absorbing the impact. After 8,000 steps — a typical Singapore commuter's daily count — that adds up.

Fashion flats are designed to feel comfortable during the 90 seconds you walk around a showroom. Not for 8,000 steps on marble.

## What actually determines whether a shoe hurts

Comfort is the result of specific design choices. Four things determine it.

**Heel height** in the 2–4 cm range offloads the heel without dangerously redistributing weight forward — and doesn't strain an Achilles that heels have already shortened.

**Arch support that holds its shape.** Memory foam compresses under sustained weight and doesn't recover — your support degrades progressively through the day. Sunnystep uses a Resilient Polyurethane Core that maintains its structure under continuous load. The support at 9am is the same at 6pm.

**Wide toe box.** Your foot spreads under load when you walk. A narrow last compresses the toes and builds forefoot pain over time. If your toes can't splay while standing in the shoe, it's already too tight for a full workday.

**Firm heel counter.** Press the back of any shoe before buying. If it collapses easily, it will fail during wear — allowing lateral rolling that travels up through the ankle and knee.
