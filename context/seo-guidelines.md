# SEO Guidelines — Sunnystep

## Market & Locale
- **Primary market**: Singapore
- **Language**: English (Singapore/British spelling — colour, minimise, organise, favour)
- **Blog path**: /blogs/ (Shopline convention — NOT /blog/)
- **Domain**: sunnystep.com

## Content Length Targets
- **Standard articles**: 1,500–2,500 words
- **Pillar/comprehensive guides**: 2,500–3,500 words
- **Quick answer / low-competition**: 800–1,200 words
- Quality over quantity — never pad to hit word counts

## Keyword Placement Rules

Primary keyword MUST appear in:
- H1 headline (preferably near the start)
- First 100 words of the article
- At least 2 H2 subheadings
- Meta title (within first 55 characters)
- Meta description
- URL slug

**Density**: 1–2% — natural usage, never stuffed

## Meta Elements

### Meta Title
- 50–60 characters
- Include primary keyword + "Singapore" for local keywords
- Example: `Most Comfortable Walking Shoes in Singapore 2026`

### Meta Description
- 140–155 characters
- Include primary keyword + a benefit hook
- Example: `Tired of sore feet by 3pm? These are Singapore's most comfortable walking shoes, backed by science and 5,000+ five-star reviews.`

### URL Slug
- Lowercase, hyphens only (no underscores)
- Include primary keyword, no dates (evergreen)
- Example: `comfortable-walking-shoes-singapore`

## Content Structure

- **H1**: One per page, contains primary keyword
- **H2s**: 4–7 sections, 2–3 should include keyword variations
- **H3s**: Subsections under H2s
- **Intro**: Hook → problem → promise → keyword in first 100 words
- **Conclusion**: Recap key points + clear CTA linking to product/collection page

## AI Search Optimisation (GEO)

- **Answer first**: State the direct answer in the first 1–2 sentences before the narrative
- **Key Takeaways block**: After intro, before first H2 — 3–5 bullet points summarising conclusions
- **FAQ section**: 4–6 questions written in natural conversational language (how real people ask)
- **One idea per H2 section**: AI models parse by section; keep sections focused

## Internal Linking
- Minimum 2–3 internal links per article (at least one to a product or collection page)
- Use descriptive anchor text — never "click here"
- Always close with a CTA linking to the most relevant product or collection page
- Reference `context/internal-links-map.md` for all URLs

## Content Pillars
1. **Foot Health & Education** — arch support, foot pain, how shoe design affects your body
2. **Shoe Buying Guides** — how to choose shoes for a specific problem or occasion
3. **Singapore Lifestyle** — shoes for commuting, office culture, humid climate
4. **Product Spotlights** — deep dives on specific Sunnystep products
5. **Style Guides** — comfortable shoes that don't sacrifice style

## What to Avoid
- Generic filler openers: "In today's fast-paced world…"
- AI clichés: "delve into", "it's worth noting", "in conclusion"
- Keyword stuffing — use natural variations
- Dates in URL slugs (breaks evergreen value)
- Linking only to the homepage — go deeper to product/collection pages
