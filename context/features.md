# Sunnystep Products & Features

## Core Value Propositions

### 1. Science-Backed Comfort
- **Feature**: Medical-grade insoles developed in collaboration with MIT Sports Lab
- **Benefit**: Clinically validated comfort — not just marketing copy
- **Conversion Angle**: "The only Singapore shoe brand that's tested its comfort with MIT."

### 2. Wide Toe Box
- **Feature**: Designed for natural foot shapes — wider than standard fashion shoes
- **Benefit**: Eliminates toe compression, reduces bunion pain, improves balance
- **Conversion Angle**: "Most shoes are designed for narrow feet. Yours probably aren't."

### 3. Acupressure Insole
- **Feature**: Built-in acupressure points for active foot stimulation
- **Benefit**: Promotes circulation and energy throughout the day
- **Conversion Angle**: "A subtle massage with every step."

### 4. Arch Support
- **Feature**: Engineered arch support in every model
- **Benefit**: Reduces stress on feet, knees, and lower back
- **Conversion Angle**: "Good shoes protect more than just your feet."

### 5. Lightweight Construction
- **Feature**: Feather-light materials across all models
- **Benefit**: No fatigue from carrying heavy shoes all day
- **Conversion Angle**: "You won't believe how light they are until you pick them up."

### 6. Sustainable Materials
- **Feature**: Cruelty-free, vegan materials; some styles use algae foam and recycled coffee grounds
- **Benefit**: Comfort without compromise — for your body or the planet
- **Conversion Angle**: "Science-led. Planet-friendly."

### 7. Versatility
- **Feature**: Modern, minimal, often unisex designs
- **Benefit**: One pair works from desk to dinner, weekday to weekend
- **Conversion Angle**: "The only shoe you need to pack for a work trip."

## Competitive Differentiators

### vs. Fashion Brands (Charles & Keith, Pedro, etc.)
- Sunnystep has genuine ergonomic engineering; fashion brands prioritise aesthetics over foot health
- Wide toe box vs. narrow fashion lasts
- MIT-validated comfort vs. unverified claims

### vs. Comfort/Orthotic Brands (Vionic, Clarks, etc.)
- Sunnystep looks modern and stylish — not clinical or "orthopaedic-looking"
- Lighter weight, more versatile
- Singapore-local brand that understands the local climate and lifestyle

### vs. Sports Brands (Adidas, Nike walking shoes)
- Sunnystep bridges the office-casual gap — sporty brands look out of place in professional settings
- More appropriate for dressy occasions

## Key Messaging for Blog Content

When writing blog articles, weave in these angles naturally:
- **Pain validation**: Acknowledge that foot pain from shoes is real and common
- **Science as trust**: Reference arch support, wide toe box, MIT collaboration as facts — not hype
- **Singapore context**: Long work days, humid climate, lots of walking (MRT + office)
- **Style-comfort myth**: Challenge the idea that you have to choose one or the other
- **CTAs**: Drive to product collection pages or specific product pages, not just the homepage
