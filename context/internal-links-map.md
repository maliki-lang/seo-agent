## Linking Rules
- Minimum per article: 2 internal links (at least one to a product or collection page)
- Anchor text: Descriptive and natural — e.g. "comfortable flats for the office" not "click here"
- CTA at end of every article: Link to the most relevant collection or product page
- Don't link to the homepage — go deeper
