# Social Research: Office Shoes That Don't Hurt

**Date**: 2026-04-23

---

## Key Insights from Podiatry Sources & Community Forums

### PSC Women & Children Clinic (Singapore-specific)
- **URL**: https://pscwomenandchildren.sg/blog/womens-foot-pain-singapore/
- **Quotable**: "without support, flats are simply heels without height" — Singapore podiatrist. No competitor article uses this.
- Singapore's office culture (finance, retail, hospitality) expects women to wear heels daily as professional attire
- Hard tile and concrete throughout Singapore malls, offices, and MRT stations means shock absorption isn't optional
- Women's anatomy is structurally mismatched with most shoes: narrower heels, wider forefeet — most shoes are designed around men's proportions
- Fluctuating estrogen levels affect ligament laxity, making women more susceptible to overpronation and arch collapse
- **"chronic pain is never 'normal,' no matter the job"** — podiatrist pushback against the normalisation of foot pain

### Tread Labs (aggregating podiatrist quotes)
- **URL**: https://treadlabs.com/blogs/insoles-reach-your-stride/comfortable-shoes-for-professional-women
- Dr. Hillary Brenner: ballet flats have "no arch support whatsoever" — compares them to cardboard
- Dr. Matthew Garoufalis: daily heel-wearing causes the Achilles tendon to "shrink" — which means switching to flats then becomes painful too (the vicious cycle no one talks about)
- Dr. Neal Blitz: "walking in high heels itself is unnatural"
- "hungover feet" — real term used for post-heel pain that lingers after removing the shoes. Very shareable.
- "uncomfortable shoes can make a long day at work much longer"

### Community/Forum Language
- Users cycle between heels and flats because flats "should be comfortable" — most don't know flats without arch support cause overpronation
- Common complaint pattern: bought supposedly comfortable shoe → felt fine in shop → painful after 3 hours
- "break-in period" normalisation: women told pain is temporary when it's actually the shoe failing them
- Frustration: finding shoes that are professional AND comfortable, especially in Asian office contexts with dress codes

---

## Pain Points Identified

1. **The no-win cycle**: heels hurt the forefoot, switching to flats hurts the arch — because Achilles has shortened from years of heel-wearing
2. **The showroom trap**: shoes designed to feel comfortable in a 90-second trial, not an 8-hour shift
3. **The "just break them in" myth**: normalised advice that actually means tolerating damage until your foot adapts
4. **Anatomy mismatch**: women's wider forefeet compressed into shoe lasts designed for men
5. **Singapore-specific**: dress code expectation of heels daily, on surfaces (MRT, marble offices) harder than Western equivalents

## Language to Use (Real User Phrases)

- "hungover feet" — post-heel pain that persists at home
- "should be comfortable" — the assumption about flats that sets people up for disappointment  
- "breaking in" — the false comfort narrative
- "comfortable in the shop" — the showroom trap
- "just wearing ballet flats" — said as if that automatically means no pain

## Language to Avoid (SEO-speak)

- "ergonomic footwear solution"
- "biomechanical optimisation"
- "podiatric support system"
- "all-day comfort technology"
